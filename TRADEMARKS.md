# Squaero trademark policy

The Squaero **code** is free software under the [GPLv3](LICENSE) (with the
[App Store permission](LICENSE-EXCEPTION)). The **name "Squaero" and its
logos** are not covered by that license: they identify the official app
published by Daniel Noé Núñez López, so that users know what they are
installing.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your software is "based on Squaero" or "a fork of Squaero".
- Use the name to talk about the project: articles, reviews, tutorials,
  videos, talks, package listings that ship the unmodified official app.
- Build and use Squaero, modified or not, for yourself or inside your
  organisation.

## What needs written permission

- Publishing a modified version — in an app store, a package repository or
  anywhere else — under the name "Squaero", or a name or icon that could be
  mistaken for it.
- Using the logos in a product, service or domain name.
- Implying that your version is official or endorsed by the project.

A fork is welcome: give it its own name and icon, keep the GPL notices and
credit the original project.

## Contact

Ask for permission or report misuse through
